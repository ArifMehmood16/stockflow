## Delivered

- ADR 002 recommends run-scoped UUID stock and leaves the numeric diagnostic catalog immutable.
- Idempotency is one transaction: unique-key claim, stock change, stored response, single commit. Insufficient stock replays the stored 409.
- The small fixture is 400 rows. Explicit `FIXTURE_ROWS` is capped by the catalog rows actually present and by disk admission. The 100,000-row example is withdrawn.
- Future shards copy a local catalog snapshot. They do not use a cross-database foreign key.
- No schema, role, token, or service code was added.
